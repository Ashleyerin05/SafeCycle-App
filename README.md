The city of Boston records hundreds of crashes annually. With bike infrastructure being integrated throughout the city, the city welcomes around 6,100 rides daily, 
leaving a market for residents who want to ride safely. This is where SafeCycle comes in. Using data of crash records, protected bike lanes, and Bluebike trips,
we created an app that directs riders on the safest path, taking traffic, construction, and bike lane obstructions into account. SafeCyle allows riders to navigate 
through Boston choosing the path that will make them the most confident. 

Safe Cycle works by, first, having riders enter their starting point and destination. From there, riders can decide on a scale how much they value speed vs safety 
while riding. Then, our app uses compiled data to figure out the route balancing what the rider is requesting.
